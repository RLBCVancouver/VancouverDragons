New to league? Start here

Returning player or picking up rugby league for the first time, you're welcome. Many of our players come from rugby union.

  1. April

    Pre-season

    Training is expected to start in April. Dates and location to come.

  2. Late May / early June

    RLBC 9s

    The season usually opens with a 9s tournament.

  3. June – July

    Regular season

    Home games at Brockton Oval, with a bye over the Canada Day long weekend.

  4. Summer

    Finals

    Semifinals, then the Grand Final to finish the season.

Coming from union?

Position names can sound the same but mean different jobs. This guide shows where union players usually fit in league. Coaches decide your final spot based on your skills, size and experience.

How do I register?

Registration runs through Rugby League BC. Fees for 2027 are set by RLBC and vary by club and competition. They typically cover the 9s tournament, regular season games and playoff fixtures.

Register with RLBC →

When does training start?

Practice for 2027 is expected to begin in April. Training dates and location are announced closer to pre-season. DM us on Instagram or email secretary@vancouverdragons.ca for details.

I play union. Can I still play league?

Yes. Many Dragons players come from rugby union, and the club was set up so you can play union in the winter and league in the summer. Dragons players have strong ties to VRRFC, Scribes, Kats, Meralomas and Burnaby Lake.
