The heart of the game

The set of six

You get six tackles to move the ball up the field. Good teams use every one with a purpose. Watch the bars at the top of this page: they count a set as you scroll.

  1. 1

    Set the platform

    Secure possession, quick play-the-ball, get go-forward.

  2. 2

    Build momentum

    Keep carrying through the middle or edges and hold your shape.

  3. 3

    Create opportunities

    Shift the defence with shape and support.

  4. 4

    Apply pressure

    Look for a mismatch, move the ball, change the angle.

  5. 5

    Set up the finish

    Get in position for a kick or an attacking play.

  6. 6

    Finish the set

    Kick for territory, take the line on, or go for points.

Questions

Before you sign up

How do I register?

Registration runs through Rugby League BC. Fees for 2027 are set by RLBC and vary by club and competition. They typically cover the 9s tournament, regular season games and playoff fixtures.

Register with RLBC →

When does training start?

Practice for 2027 is expected to begin in April. Training dates and location are announced closer to pre-season. DM us on Instagram or email secretary@vancouverdragons.ca for details.

Who's in the league?

Five clubs compete in RLBC for 2027:

  • Point Grey Thunder
  • Valley Warriors
  • Vancouver Dragons
  • Vancouver Valley Vipers
  • Whistler Wolves
I play union. Can I still play league?

Yes. Many Dragons players come from rugby union, and the club was set up so you can play union in the winter and league in the summer. Dragons players have strong ties to VRRFC, Scribes, Kats, Meralomas and Burnaby Lake.

Can international players join?

Yes. There are no restrictions on international players. The club is fully amateur, so there is no payment, but we do have contacts for work and accommodation in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.

You need your own playing health insurance (MSP, or travel insurance that covers amateur sport), as Canada Rugby League's player insurance requires it. More at rugbyleague.ca.

Can I help without playing?

We're a family-oriented club of ex-pats from rugby league nations and new Canadian families who have taken to the sport. We always need volunteers for home game duties, team management and the jobs it takes to run a club.
