Schedule

Rugby League BC is finalising the 2027 fixtures. The full schedule goes up here as soon as it's confirmed.

⏳2027 schedule in progress. Dates and venues haven't been officially confirmed yet. Follow @vancouver_dragons_ for the announcement.

How the season runs

  1. April

    Pre-season

    Training is expected to start in April. Dates and location to come.

  2. Late May / early June

    RLBC 9s

    The season usually opens with a 9s tournament.

  3. June – July

    Regular season

    Home games at Brockton Oval, with a bye over the Canada Day long weekend.

  4. Summer

    Finals

    Semifinals, then the Grand Final to finish the season.

Results, standings and registration are on the Rugby League BC website.
