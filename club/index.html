Vancouver Dragons Rugby League Football Club

Our own club since 2012

Founded by players with a passion for rugby league, playing out of Brockton Oval in Stanley Park.

The story

The Vancouver Dragons were formed in 2012 by expats from the southern hemisphere. The club played its first competition at the inaugural 2013 RLBC International 9's in Squamish, then joined the 2013 RLBC season with home games at Brockton Oval in Stanley Park.

The founders chose to build their own club rather than attach to a traditional Vancouver rugby union side. That lets players turn out for their union club in the winter and the Dragons in the summer. Dragons players have strong ties to VRRFC, Scribes RFC, Kats RFC, Meralomas RFC and Burnaby Lake RFC, alongside anyone in Vancouver who loves the game.

Today we're a family-oriented club of ex-pats from rugby league nations and new Canadian families who have embraced the sport.

Milestones

  1. 2012

    Club formed in Vancouver.

  2. 2013

    First competition at the inaugural RLBC International 9's in Squamish, then the first RLBC season at Brockton Oval.

  3. 2018

    A historic treble: RLBC Men's Premier champions, RLBC 9s champions and Vancouver City Touch champions. Watch the Grand Final →

  4. 2020–21

    No competition during the pandemic, then a restricted return to training.

  5. 2026

    RLBC 9s champions, beating Valley Vipers in the final at Brockton Oval. The Reserves also won the first-ever RLBC Reserve Grade championship.

  6. 2027

    Back to Las Vegas for the NRL Vegas 9's in February. Back the trip →
